Architecture

Six systems.
Working as one.

01
Pose Classification

A Random Forest model (200 estimators) trained on 7,200 synthetic samples identifies which of 18 poses the instructor is performing. 98.7% accuracy with a 45% confidence gate — below which it falls back to rule-based classification.

pose_classifier.py
02
Angle Comparison

8 joint angles are extracted from both you and the instructor using a dot-product formula. Each misaligned joint is flagged with its direction (increase/decrease) and degree of error. Joints below 50% landmark visibility are excluded.

angle_calculator.py
03
Similarity Scoring

A weighted cosine angular similarity score from 0–100% is computed. Hips and knees carry 1.5× weight; shoulders 1.2×. Below 75% → corrections given. Above 85% → positive praise. The 75% threshold is backed by three peer-reviewed papers.

similarity_scorer.py
04
Correction AI

An MLP neural network (57 features → 128→64→32 → 141 corrections) selects the most appropriate coaching cue. It knows that a Warrior II knee correction is completely different from a Chair pose knee correction — no hardcoded rules.

correction_ai.pkl
05
State Machine

Three states — TRACKING, FROZEN, CATCHING UP — handle the gap between instructor pace and your pace. When the instructor moves on but you haven't held the pose yet, the system locks the previous pose as your target for up to 45 seconds.

pose_state_machine.py
06
Neural TTS Voice

Microsoft Edge Neural TTS (JennyNeural and 14 other language voices) speaks corrections asynchronously on a background thread. The main loop never waits for audio. Fallback chain: edge-tts → pyttsx3 → OS speech → console print.

audio_feedback.py
The ML Model

141 corrections.
57 features. One choice.

Every frame, the Correction AI encodes your context into 57 numbers and predicts the single most appropriate coaching phrase — with a confidence score attached.

12,672 Training Examples

Synthetically generated by exhaustively covering every pose × joint × direction × severity combination. Deterministic ground truth means 100% top-1 test accuracy.

57 Input Features

Pose one-hot (19), joint one-hot (9), direction, angle diff, severity band, bilateral flag, secondary joint, score, body region, diff band, pose group.

100% Test Accuracy

Not overfitting — the training data is deterministic. Same context always maps to same correction. The model learned a clean, perfectly separable function.
