## 1. WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**The One-Sentence Pitch**
This is a real-time AI yoga coaching system that now features an always-on-top desktop overlay and multilingual feedback. You open a yoga video on your screen and stand in front of your laptop webcam. The system watches both you and the instructor simultaneously, compares your joint angles using MediaPipe pose estimation, calculates a match percentage, and gives you specific spoken corrections in over 14 languages. A dynamic "Ghost Skeleton" visual overlay projects the instructor's body as a colour-coded stencil directly over yours so you can see exactly what to adjust. No gym equipment or wearables. Just a laptop.

**What Makes It Different From Other Yoga Apps**
- Most yoga apps just show you a video. This one watches you back.
- The feedback is specific: not just 'fix your knee' but 'Warrior Two lives in the legs — sink deeper into that left knee.'
- It translates these complex biomechanical prompts into multiple languages seamlessly.
- It operates fully decoupled from any streaming platform (Zoom, YouTube).

## 5. HOW THE ML MODEL WAS TRAINED

We utilized a **Random Forest** over Neural Networks because:
1. The feature space is small (24 numbers — joint angles and positional ratios).
2. It trains in under 5 seconds (no heavy GPUs needed).
3. Highly interpretable — you can see exactly which joint broke the pose structure.

## 6. THE 75% THRESHOLD — RESEARCH & CITATIONS

The 75% threshold means: the user must achieve a weighted similarity score of at least 75% compared to the instructor's pose before the system praises them.

Why 75%? Three published papers converge on this:
1. **Nagarkar et al. (2022):** Recommends ≥75% weighted angular similarity as "correctly performed".
2. **Srivastava et al. (2023):** Uses 0.75 cosine similarity as the acceptance threshold.
3. **Thoutam et al. (2021):** Identifies 70-80% angular similarity band as safe.

---

## 7. THE SIMILARITY SCORE FORMULA — DEEP DIVE

**Formula:**
`Per-joint score = max(0, 1 − |user_angle − ref_angle| / 180)`
`Weighted total = Σ(weight_j × s_j) / Σ(weight_j) × 100`

Joints have unique weightings dictated in `config.py`:
- **Hips (1.5):** Drive the fundamental center of mass.
- **Knees (1.3):** Controls lunge depth and load bearing.
- **Shoulders/Elbows (1.0/1.2):** Determine alignment but are less prone to injury.

## 8. THE VOICE MODEL & MULTILINGUAL CAPABILITIES

**What is edge-tts?**
An open-source Python package connecting to Microsoft's Azure Cognitive Services. 
Using the **JennyNeural** conversational AI voice, it ensures feedback doesn't sound robotic but rather like a human coach.

**How Translations Are Handled (The 2026 Update)**
We solved the problem of global accessibility by decoupling English logic from the voice engine:
1. `feedback_builder.py` formulates the core string (e.g., "Straighten your left arm").
2. `language_manager.py` intercepts this string. It cross-references `language_setting.json` (e.g., set to "hi" for Hindi).
3. It instantly pulls the correct local string from `languages/translations_hi.json`.
4. `audio_feedback.py` adjusts the Edge TTS playback voice (e.g., `hi-IN-SwaraNeural`) to perfectly match the local phonetic cadences. 

This happens asynchronously without blocking the visual processing loop.

## 9. MEDIAPIPE — HOW POSE DETECTION WORKS

MediaPipe uses a two-stage pipeline:
1. **BlazePose Detector:** Locates the human bounding box.
2. **BlazePose Landmark Model:** Predicts 33 joint positions (`x`, `y`, `z` depth, and `visibility` score).

Angles are computed using the dot product of two vectors stretching across three joints. Only main hinge and ball/socket joints (Knees, Elbows, Shoulders, Hips, Ankles) are actively filtered for calculation.

## 10. THE STATE MACHINE — TRACKING / FROZEN / CATCHING UP

Yoga videos do not pause for you. 
If the instructor skips to Downward Dog while you are still stabilizing in Plank, the system invokes the **State Machine** in `pose_state_machine.py`.

- **TRACKING:** Normal sync.
- **FROZEN:** Instructor advanced, but user hasn't achieved the 8-second 75% score required. The system locks onto the previous pose and continues coaching.
- **CATCHING UP:** User fulfills the locked sequence and seamlessly re-syncs back to the live video frame.

## 11. THE BODY STENCIL — HOW THE OVERLAY WORKS

Instead of fixed windows, we use a dynamic Ghost Skeleton stencil.

**How the Body-Alignment Works:**
1. The relative shoulder breadth `dist = |L_shoulder - R_shoulder|` is measured for both instructor and user.
2. We derive Scale X and Scale Y values. 
3. The instructor's skeleton is actively transformed onto the user's coordinate space.

**Colour Coding Matrix:**
- 🟩 **Green:** Matched (Delta < 20°)
- 🟦 **Cyan:** Very close
- 🟧 **Orange:** Misaligned (Adjust)
- 🟥 **Red:** Critical Error (High priority fix)

## 12. NATURAL VOICE COACHING — HOW FEEDBACK IS BUILT

A multi-layered priority queue decides what to say:
1. **Combined:** If two knees are wrong → "Sink into both knees equally."
2. **Pose Specific:** In Warrior II → "Warrior Two lives in the legs — sink deeper into that left knee."
3. **Generic:** "Bend your left elbow."

If differences are severe (>35°), the tone is extremely direct. If close (<15°), the engine randomly interjects encouragement like *"Almost there."*

## 15. LIKELY QUESTIONS YOU WILL BE ASKED

**Q: What happens if there's no internet?**
A: `edge-tts` fails over automatically to `pyttsx3`, leveraging your native OS SAPI5 text-to-speech. You lose the human-like tone, but functionality is preserved offline. The translation maps are pre-downloaded JSON files, so they never break.

**Q: Why 5 FPS processing instead of 30 FPS?**
A: Yoga transitions are incredibly slow. Pushing BlazePose to 30 FPS consumes 80%+ of an Intel i5 CPU resulting in thermal throttling and lagging Zoom frames. 5 FPS keeps the system lightweight and practically invisible to the OS load.

**Q: What if the user stands sideways causing occlusion?**
A: Our `CameraChecker` module implements a Z-Depth ambiguity fail-safe. If `<8` key joints exhibit a visibility score > 0.6 due to the user pointing the crown of their head to the camera, the Ghost Skeleton shuts down rendering briefly to prevent hallucinating coordinates.
